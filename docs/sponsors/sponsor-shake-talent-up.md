- She is the room's talent router: matching standout hackers to her startup clients' open roles (Greek startups + scale-ups above).
- Expect: CV/portfolio collection point, open-roles board (physical or Discord), possibly speed-intros at the venue; finalists get routed to her clients first.
- Her posted roles skew non-engineering too (performance marketing etc.) — but for this room, eng/agent-builder roles at client startups are the relevant lane.

## 3. What they want

- **Deal-flow, but for people**: first look at 100 vetted builders before any employer gets to them. Candidates = inventory she can place into Welcome Pickups/HarborLab-orbit clients.
- Brand: positioning as the human-centered recruiter for Athens startups; the "conscious placement" pitch is her differentiator vs volume recruiters.
- Content/community: Hiring Meetup (Nov 26) signups — likely cross-promoted to participants.

## 4. How to win them

- If job-curious: give her something routable — a one-line "who you are + what you built this weekend" makes her job easy. Your demo IS the interview.
- Founders in the room: she's a backchannel into other teams' talent; hiring a strong teammate mid-hackathon is a legitimate play.
- Referencing her own language ("right people, right roles, right humans") signals you read the room.

**Bottom line:** smallest sponsor by headcount, highest leverage per conversation — she's the door into a dozen Greek startups' hiring pipelines. If you want an Athens tech job, this table outranks every VC in the room.
