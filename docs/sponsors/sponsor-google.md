# Sponsor Teardown: Google (google.com)

**Recon date:** 2026-11-14 · **Event:** Gen AI Summit Hackathon, Athens, 14–15 Nov 2026
**Why it matters:** biggest logo on the partner list, added ~Nov 12 with Cloud Office + SHAKE. On /credits: **"Google AI — AI inference — Everyone — TBA"** (https://hackathon.polymath42.com/credits). Almost certainly a Technology Partner (in-kind credits), not cash.

## 1. Why Google is here — the Athens week connection

- **Google Cloud Day Athens was Nov 10, 2026** (Athens Conservatoire) — 4 days before the hackathon. The Google Cloud Greece/Cyprus team + DevRel are physically in town this exact week. Sponsor roster overlap is not a coincidence. Source: https://cloud.google.com/events/google-cloud-day-athens-2026
- Strategic backdrop: Google announced **Greece's first Google Cloud Region** Sept 2022 — ~€2.2B GDP contribution, ~20k jobs by 2030, PM Mitsotakis attended the launch — plus two Centers of Excellence w/ Deloitte (Thessaloniki = AI, Patras = sustainability). Sources: https://www.reuters.com/technology/google-build-its-first-cloud-region-greece-2022-09-29/, https://www.primeminister.gr/en/2022/09/29/30303
- Cloud Office (also a new partner this delta) is the **only Premier Google Cloud Partner in the region** and is actively building its Athens office — Google + Cloud Office arriving together reads as a coordinated Google-Greece-ecosystem push.

## 2. What they likely put on the table

- **"AI inference" credits = Gemini API** (AI Studio free tier / paid keys, possibly Vertex AI express mode or $ Google Cloud credits). Claim mechanics still TBA on /credits — check the page at kickoff; expect an AI Studio link or a coupon code.
- Hackable Google stack (name-checkable):
  - **Gemini API / AI Studio** — flash-class models, generous free tier, `google-genai` SDK, structured output, function calling, grounding w/ Google Search.
  - **ADK (Agent Development Kit)** + **google-agents-cli** — literally demoed at Cloud Day Athens this week ("mkdir agent && deploy" — blank terminal to deployed agent in 25 min, Juraj Gvoth).
  - **Vertex AI Agent Engine / Agent Builder** for deployed-agent framing.
  - Gemini Live API for voice/multimodal; CodeMender (autonomous patch agent) also demoed at Cloud Day.
- Building visibly on Gemini + ADK = the usage metric they report back.

## 4. What they want

- Gemini API + ADK adoption and the "agents built on Google stack" story for the SEE ecosystem.
- Developer mindshare where Anthropic/OpenAI dominate agent builds — this room is 100 pre-screened agent builders, a cheap audience.
- Ecosystem optics: Cloud Region + Cloud Office expansion = Google investing visibly in Greek developers.

## 5. How to win them

- **Dual-run Gemini Flash alongside your frontier model** — route a cheap subtask (classification, summarization, routing) to Gemini; say "we benchmarked it" on stage.
- **Use ADK or google-agents-cli somewhere real** — even one sub-agent deployed through their toolchain gives them a quotable artifact.
- Speak Cloud Region: "built it on the stack Google is literally standing up in Greece."
- Cheap aura: ground a research agent with Google Search grounding — the demo shows live citations, it's their differentiator.

**Bottom line:** Google didn't write a check, it shipped credits + people. Winning them is usage theater: Gemini and/or ADK visibly inside the agent, plus a named comparison vs the default frontier API.
