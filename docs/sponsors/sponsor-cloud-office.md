# Sponsor Teardown: Cloud Office (cloud-office.com)

**Recon date:** 2026-11-14 · **Event:** Gen AI Summit Hackathon, Athens, 14–15 Nov 2026
**Why it matters:** added ~Nov 12 alongside Google + SHAKE. Google Cloud's Premier Partner for the region — almost certainly the conduit behind Google's own sponsorship. **Not on /credits** — they're here for hiring + market entry, not usage.

## 1. Who they are

- Cloud consultancy/integrator, founded **2019, Sofia HQ**; ~**88 staff, +72.5% YoY** (LinkedIn). Offices: Sofia (HQ), **Athens–Marousi (Astronafton 1 & Stavroulaki)**, London, Nicosia (4th office, opened Jul 2026).
- **Funding: €2M seed, Nov 2024, Eleven Ventures** (SEE fund; also backed Greek Moveo.AI). Dealroom EV est. $9–13M.
- **Only Premier Google Cloud Partner in the region holding 3 Specializations** (Infrastructure, Work Transformation, Data Analytics); also AWS Solution Provider + JumpCloud/GitLab/Acronis partner. Regional Google Cloud Partner of the Year 2024. 400+ delivered projects, 650–800+ customers (fintech, iGaming, SaaS, telco, media).
- Founders (ex-Sutherland/ScaleFocus/Google colleagues): **Ivo Zimbilev** (CEO), **Dimitar Pavlov** (CCO), **Ivan Dobrinov** (CTO), **Chavdar Trendafilov** (Head of Customer Engineering), **Georgi Gocev** (Head of Service Delivery), **Dimitar Chakarov** (Alliances Director). Sources: https://www.cloud-office.com/about-us, https://www.11.vc/eleven-invests-in-cloud-office/, https://www.vestbee.com/insights/articles/cloud-office-secures-2-m-from-eleven-ventures

## 2. Greece presence — the reason they sponsored

- The €2M round was raised **explicitly to expand into Greece, where "no established Google Cloud partners operate"** (their words). Athens office's first full year = 2025.
- **Greek clients already: Antenna Group, Dialectica, Athens Exchange Group** (per Country Head's year-end post).
- Greece team: **Konstantinos Kalogerakis — Country Head, Cloud Office Greece** (now also oversees Cyprus; LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/kkalogerakis); **Aikaterini Siomopoulou** — Head of Sales; engineers Fivos Simeonidis, Theodoros-Loukas Syrogiannis, Ermis Doganis; **Stelios Papantoniou** (tagged in their roster, also appears under Google colors at Cloud Day — ex-Google hire or joint cover, ask him).
- **Actively hiring Athens engineers** (data engineer BigQuery/Python, cloud engineer GKE/Terraform — posted Feb 2026). A hackathon = exactly the sourcing funnel they need.
- Ran their own Athens event "AI @ Work: Tools That Help vs. Agents That Act" (Apr 2026, Athens Olympic Museum) w/ Google + JumpCloud speakers; **Victor Tchervenobrejki, Head of AI & ML Solutions**, led the agentic-workflows session — likely the most technical person on their table.

## 3. Relevant surface for hackers

- No credits listed. Their value on-site: GCP/Gemini/Vertex implementation know-how, BigQuery/agent-stack advice, **jobs**.
- Their pitch to you: "we put agents into production for real enterprises — come talk." If your project looks production-shaped (identity, auditability, SRE-flavored ops), you're speaking their portfolio language.

## 4. What they want

- **Talent** — Athens team growing fast; 100 screened builders is a hiring pipeline, not a market.
- **Market entry visibility** — year-two push into Greek enterprise; sponsor logo buys brand with the exact founder/startup audience that becomes clients (or employees' next employers).
- **Google alignment** — usage of GCP/Gemini in the room validates the partnership they sell.

## 5. How to win them

- Treat them as an interviewer, not a judge: if you're job-curious, a production-grade demo (Terraform-able, audited, costed) IS the CV.
- Name-check Google Cloud primitives correctly (Vertex AI Agent Engine, GKE, BigQuery, Workspace flows) — mislabeling Google products in front of a Premier Partner is anti-aura.
- Reference their own framing: "agents that act" + "organizational truth" (grounded on company data) — repeat those phrases back with your demo as proof.
- Ask Kalogerakis about the Cyprus expansion and the open Athens roles — flattery through informed questions.

**Bottom line:** Cloud Office is the stealth employer of the event. Their prize is a conversation with the best builders in the room; play to that if Athens-based GCP/agentic work interests you.
