## 3. The ISP machine — and how to compress it into 5 minutes

**Format (verified):** Monthly ISP, 10–15 candidates. Each candidate: **3 × 1-hour interviews with pairs of panelists** (6 interviewers total — accomplished founders, investors, C-levels). Panelists pre-study the file. **Unanimous yes from all six** = Endeavor Entrepreneur. Global hit rate **~1.6–2%** (2024: 81 companies selected worldwide; network now 3,136 entrepreneurs / 2,105 companies). Funnel: staff First Opinion → 3–5 mentor Second Opinion Reviews → Local Selection Panel (unanimous) → ISP (unanimous). 94th ISP was held in Athens (Jul 2022).

**The Selection Triangle (Endeavor's own terms):**
- **ENTREPRENEUR** — "leaders capable of inspiring generations." Ambition, transparency, openness, capability = non-negotiables. Must be a future *multiplier*: "dream big, scale fast, pay it forward." The "who" sits at the center of the triangle; charisma without substance fails, and bluffing fails the transparency test.
- **BUSINESS** — scale fast, create large-scale wealth + jobs, be defensible. Numbers, not vision slides.
- **TIMING** — at an inflection point, "priming for blitzscaling"; can it credibly 10x from here? Founders think future, panelists ground in present: "does today's reality match the vision?"

**Compressed into a 5-min pitch (map, don't recite):**
1. **0:00–0:30 — Demo cold-open.** Hackathon mandate: "not a pitching competition, it's an agent at work." Show the agent doing real work end-to-end before any words about you.
2. **0:30–1:30 — ENTREPRENEUR.** Founder-market fit: why *you* are obsessed with this problem; one line signaling multiplier instinct (what you'll give back / open-source / hire-in-Athens).
3. **1:30–3:00 — BUSINESS.** The 10x story: named user pain → market wedge → why it scales → defensibility (moat, data, distribution). One traction/honesty number beats three TAM slides.
4. **3:00–3:45 — TIMING.** Why now is the inflection: agents moving "out of the demo and into the job" (the summit's own theme, "Agents at Work" — echo it back).
5. **3:45–5:00 — Demo payoff + closer.** Finish the live agent run; close on the Endeavor-shaped sentence: "this can be a company, we're Greek-linked/global-first, and here's what paying it forward looks like."
Kill shots: hype claims you can't verify, Greece-only TAM, hiding weaknesses, solo-founder-does-everything framing (they select leaders who attract followership).

## 5. Catalyst Fund V — what $70M Greek money signals

- **$320M closed** (announced globally ~Oct 7, 2026; Greek coverage Nov 2) — oversubscribed vs $300M target, largest fund yet. AUM now **>$850M**; 437 portfolio cos / 44 markets / **83 unicorns**.
- **Greece = #1 LP market:** **$70M from 17 Greek investors ≈ 22%** of the fund — biggest single-market share of 40+. Trajectory: $5M (Fund III 2019) → $24M (IV 2022) → $70M (V); cumulative Greek commitments ≈ $100M. The 17: all four systemic banks (**Alpha, Eurobank, NBG, Piraeus**) + unnamed "prominent Greek and diaspora business families" (board-adjacent families are the obvious inference — unverified by name).
- **Mechanics:** rules-based co-investor — joins rounds of Endeavor-entrepreneur companies led by institutional VCs on identical terms, ~**$1–3M checks**; ~**30% of Fund V's LPs are Endeavor Entrepreneurs themselves** (the Multiplier Effect made literal). Plans 40–50 investments/yr; already >$55M into ~30 cos (Replit, Preply, Cashea, Arena…).
- **Greek-founder book:** **$25M into 15 companies with Greek-origin founders** — 5 in the decade to 2022, 10 in the four years since (Reflection, Runway, Arena, ElevenLabs-adjacent rounds led by Nvidia/General Atlantic). Greece was Endeavor's **#2 office worldwide in Catalyst fundraising**.
- **Read-through:** Greek institutional capital has moved from skeptical→lead-LP in 6 years; banks now *need* venture exposure and Endeavor is the conduit. For a pitch: the room's money is global-first; "Greek wedge, global TAM" is the only acceptable framing. And the flywheel is explicit: get selected → Catalyst co-invests your next round → you LP the next fund.

## 7. Their founder-evaluation lens applied to us

Assume Felix (and any Endeavor-adjacent judge) is silently running the ISP triangle, not the VC checklist:
- **Pass "Entrepreneur" first:** lead with why-you, be transparent about what the agent *can't* do yet — Endeavor punishes bluffing harder than weakness. Welcome the grilling; openness-to-feedback is literally a scored trait.
- **10x, stated plainly:** name the company this becomes ("this is not a hackathon toy, it's the wedge into X — a 10x business because…"). Felix's mandate is founders who can *become companies*.
- **Greece link = his filter:** say the Athens/Greek-founder/diaspora/Greek-market line out loud; "connection to Greece" is literally his selection criterion.
- **Timing hook ready-made:** the summit's theme IS the Endeavor Timing side — agents leaving demo-land for real work. Show a real-work artifact (sent emails, filed tickets, merged PRs), not a promise.
- **Multiplier closer:** one sentence of pay-it-forward (open-sourcing the eval harness, hiring in Athens, mentoring next cohort) costs nothing and hits their core dogma.
- **Ask:** nothing crass — the right move is making yourself legible to the Scale Up / EE pipeline: "we'd want to be the kind of company Felix brings to an ISP in 18 months."
