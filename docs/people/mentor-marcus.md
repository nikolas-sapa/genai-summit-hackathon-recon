# Mentor Dossier — Eliot Marcus (Head of Agentic Commerce, Europe, Visa)

**Added to speakers-2026 ~Nov 12**. The only speaker whose title literally contains "Agentic" besides Kroupis — owns agent payments for a continent.

## Role
- **Head of Agentic Commerce, Europe @ Visa** — bio verbatim: "leads Visa's efforts in Europe to enable agentic services to pay on behalf of cardholders, in a way that is safe, secure and seamless." That is Visa Intelligent Commerce: tokenized card credentials scoped to agents — spend limits, merchant-category controls, revocable delegation — launched with OpenAI/Anthropic/Perplexity/Mistral partners.
- ~decade in payments. Prior: **headed global core network product management for Mastercard**. Earlier: asset-finance startup leadership (Zambia), SME investment fund expansion (Ghana), environmental NGO (China) — operator not career-corporate.

## What he actually builds
- The trust/delegation layer for agents that transact: how does an agent prove authority to spend, how does a merchant know it's a sanctioned agent not a bot, how do liability and disputes work when no human clicked "buy."
- Product strategy + ecosystem rollout — more product lead than IC engineer, but THE domain expert on agent-initiated transactions.

## Agent expertise (1 line)
Agent payments = the missing "last mile" of useful agents; he owns the protocol-level answers (delegated auth, spend controls, agent identity) Europe-side.

## Ask him for (hard-won intel)
- **How agent authority/delegation should work**: consent scopes, spend caps, revocation — if your demo has an agent that *transacts* (books, buys, subscribes), his model of "safe delegation" is the framework judges haven't heard yet.
- What merchant-side agent detection looks like — how Visa distinguishes good agents from fraud bots; an "agent-identity" beat makes a demo memorable.
- Mastercard network-product years = the two-sided-marketplace playbook for agents (who pays, who trusts, who's liable).
- Timing: agentic commerce is the freshest mainstream agent narrative — ask what use-cases he's actually seeing pilots for, not the PR version.

## How to work him
- Product-strategy person, not hands-on coder — use him for the "agents that act economically" design layer and pitch framing, not debugging. If your build includes any transaction/payment/procurement step (pairs well with treg's tool-procurement angle), his 10 minutes is worth more than any infra mentor's.
