## 6. Hackathon playbook — what wins *him* over

The event's stated criteria are **usefulness, craft, and "aura points"** — but if Apostolakis is judging/mentoring, his private scorecard is predictable:

1. **An agent doing real work end-to-end** — his stated AI interest is exactly this: automating tedious multi-step tasks (freight requests, service tickets, interviews, treasury, payroll). A slick chat wrapper dies; an agent that completes a workflow lives.
2. **Name the vertical and the market size** — "large markets" is his literal filter. Frame TAM beyond Greece/EU; small-market ambition is a self-admitted "no-go." Bonus: pick his wheelhouse — fintech ops, logistics, marketplaces, sales/customer service, shipping, travel, energy.
3. **Show the moat is distribution, not the model** — his 2026 thesis is that moats moved to distribution + AI-first execution. Have a credible "how we get users" story: channel, wedge, network effect, or marketplace liquidity angle.
4. **Founder energy audit** — he's scanning for mission-driven, self-motivated people with "an uncompromising need to prove they are right." Pitch with conviction and urgency ("relentless and fast execution" is his phrase); complementarity in the team registers (he looks for it explicitly).
5. **Business viability in one breath** — ex-marketplace founder + fintech-heavy VC: he'll mentally ask "who pays and why now." Have an answer even if loose. Don't overclaim B2B growth — he publicly dings inflated projections.
6. **Ambition framed globally from day one** — "global product" is his stated ingredient #1. If it's Greece-only, frame Greece as the beachhead.
7. **Craft > polish** — he's a civil-engineer-turned-operator who survived a near-bankruptcy; a working scrappy demo that does the full loop beats a beautiful slide. "Fail fast" credibility: if you pivoted during the 36h, say so — he prizes pivot-flexible teams.
8. Optional resonance hooks: reference his portfolio analogs (NeuralTrust for agent governance, CubeAI for logistics agents, Merx for conversational commerce, Valeria for workforce ops) — shows you did the homework.

## 7. Landmines / watch-outs

- **Not a sponsor** — no prize money behind him; value = access to VF dealflow + the Summit network. If he asks for your contact, that's a pre-seed signal — VF's site form gets "a reply to everyone," but in-person > cold.

- Don't present a Greece-only opportunity as the endgame.
- He respects failure stories — mentioning what you tried and killed in the 36h plays better than hiding it (his own brand is built on the e-shop near-collapse).
