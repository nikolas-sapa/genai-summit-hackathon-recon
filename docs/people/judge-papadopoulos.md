# Deep Dossier: Panos Papadopoulos — Judge candidate (Marathon VC)
## Gen AI Summit Hackathon — Athens, 14–15 Nov 2026

**Why he's on this list:** publicly volunteered on Ion Petropoulos's original Sept-13 organizing post (one of the named judge/mentor volunteers per Nov-11 delta); on Panathēnea's official mentor bench; **Marathon VC's `?sponsor=marathon` deck overlay renders a logo-watermarked proposal** (Nov-12 delta — deal in progress, not on public partner list); Marathon led the seed of NOFire (hackathon sponsor-deck slug). Top judge candidate per Nov-11/12 deltas.

## Role/employer/country
- **Partner, Marathon Venture Capital** (Mar 2017–), Athens — the flagship Greek seed-stage fund (Netdata, Lenses, Hack The Box, LearnWorlds, Lambda Automata/Delian, Harbor Lab-adjacent ecosystem). Board member, Delian Alliance Industries. Endeavor Greece mentor. Athens, Greece. LinkedIn ~18.7k followers (`panayiotis`) — one of the most-followed Greek VCs.

## Background
- **Founder before VC:** co-founded **BugSense** (2011, SF/Athens) — mobile crash/performance SDK, top-5 SDK for mobile devs, **400M+ device installs**, Fortune 500 customers; acquired by **Splunk** Sep 2013; stayed as Director of Product Management at Splunk. First company as a student: StayInAthens ("Airbnb for exchange students"). Raised in Sparta.
- Angel: Pollfish, Transifex, TradingView, Nurx and others. Co-organizes developer communities; studies crypto projects (MSc Digital Currencies, University of Nicosia; BSc Computer Science AUEB).
- At Marathon (with George Tziralis) since Fund I 2017; Fund II €40M; Marathon portfolio enterprise value historically $350M+ and much larger now (Delian, Netdata unicorns-adjacent).

## Public theses (verbatim hooks)
- Marathon bio: *"My cause is startups… Getting your startup acquired by a red hot NASDAQ-listed company is an experience that gives you a complete perspective of the startup journey."*

- Marathon blog: *"Greek founders are my tribe"*; values recruiting help — *"companies have or plan to have headcount in Greece."*
- LinkedIn bio: invests across sectors with **DevTools as his favorite** — developer tooling, infra, crypto, data, cloud infra, IoT.

## Presence

## Wins him over
Engineer-founder-investor — he reads code-level competence and GTM realism in one pass. DevTools/infra-flavored agents are his sweet spot (his stated favorite sector): an agent that itself is a developer tool, or built with a clean tool-use architecture, speaks his language. Market-validation thinking: even a 36-hour-old artifact of demand (someone asked for it, a waitlist, a named workflow owner) beats vision. Founders who built because *they* had the problem — his stated preference. Don't pitch TAM; pitch the wedge and the user. Bonus: crypto/attestation angles are a known interest of his personally — agent payments/verification could intrigue. Skeptical of trend-chasing — "we don't like trends" is his line.
