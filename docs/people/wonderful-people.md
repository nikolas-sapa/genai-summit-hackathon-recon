## 4. THE FDE EVAL LOOP (= what the hackathon silently replicates)
From Glassdoor/LeetCode candidate reports (Oct–Nov 2026, Rome/London/Athens pipelines):
1. **Recruiter screen** (~15 min, non-technical) → 2. **Live technical/coding** (Python practicals — e.g. "design an exponential-backoff helper"; LLM-flavored live AI coding) → 3. **Decomposition/scoping round** ("decomp": vague business problem → who is the user, what data exists, success metric, what ships in weeks) → 4. **Case study** → 5. **Product demo** → 6. **"Vibe checks"** — CTO call, founder call, **GM call (final veto on seniority/culture)**.
- Real reported Qs: exponential-backoff helper design; **"how do you handle a client when a demo breaks live"** — literally the hackathon scenario.
- JD signals: 5+ yrs, APIs/MCP/integrations/distributed systems, "structure and momentum to ambiguous problems," LLM eval/observability nice-to-have. DS profile: consultants/founders/early-startup operators measured on customer P&L.
- **The mapping:** scoping round ≈ choosing + slicing your build at kickoff; coding round ≈ the overnight; product demo ≈ the pitch; vibe checks ≈ table conversations. Candidates report process can run long/disorganized — a strong hackathon showing *shortcuts* the loop entirely (Savaidis is the precedent: hired at ~20 as an undergrad).

**Words that land:** containment rate, AHT, weeks-to-production, handover, on-prem-ready, process redesign, Greek-first. **Words that kill:** copilot, assistant, MVP, wrapper, "AI-powered", unmeasured claims, SMB framing.
