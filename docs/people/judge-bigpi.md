## 3. What Makes a Big-Pi-Judge Care (synthesized playbook)

Hackathon judging criteria (official): **usefulness, craft, aura points** — "agents doing real work, the tedious multi-step jobs nobody wants to do twice." Theme is deployment/ROI, not demos.

From Nick's own writing/investments, he will pattern-match to:
1. **Agent that completes real work end-to-end** — tool use, multi-step, verifiable outcome. He funds "impossible-to-automate tasks now automated" (Acumino). A thin wrapper demo = the "unreliable demos without customer value" he explicitly passes on.
2. **A moat story, even embryonic** — proprietary/hard-to-replicate data, domain depth, evals showing reliability. His robotics thesis: highest-quality real-world data wins. For agents: unique data flywheel or workflow access, not prompt cleverness.
3. **ROI/user-value framing** — "who pays, for what, how much saved." Summit theme is literally "deployment, ROI, governance." One sentence of unit economics beats five slides of vibes.
4. **Greece/global duality** — team that could build in Greece and sell globally. Bonus for solving a real Greek/SEE problem with global TAM (e.g., Prosperty-style proptech, maritime, tourism, public admin).
5. **Craft signals he notices** — works live under demo conditions, honest about failure modes, engineers who can discuss evals/architecture. He co-hosts a technical podcast; CS-degree founder; will probe.
6. **Humility > hype** — his stated pet peeve. "Ego is a liability." No AGI-bro pitch; precise claims, real limitations named. "Aura points" ≠ arrogance.

**Prosperty overlap play:** an agentic build on property data/transactions (they have APIs, auction data, AVM) flatters a sponsor AND lands in a Big Pi portfolio vertical — highest-leverage sponsor prize + judge attention combo.
