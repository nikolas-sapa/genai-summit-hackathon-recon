# Deep Dossier — 100mentors / Yiorgos Nikoletakis / Wiserwork
**Context:** Gen AI Summit Hackathon, Athens LifeTech Park, 14–15 Nov 2026. Finals on GenAI Summit 5 main stage at SNFCC, Mon 16 Nov. 100mentors is co-host of the hackathon AND sole producer of the entire summit. Companion to `../judges.md` (individual judge profiles) and `../MASTER-BRIEF.md`.

## 1. 100mentors — the org map

### What it is now vs. what it was
- **Founded May 2014** in Athens (2 yrs of prep prior) as an **EdTech SaaS mentoring platform**: connected students to vetted professional mentors via live video. Peak stats: 21,000 students, 270 schools, 17 countries, 2,500 mentors from 800+ orgs. Seed-funded ~€900k total by **OpenFund I/II** (JEREMIE/EIF-backed): $200k Oct 2014, €200k Jul 2015, ~€500k Nov 2016. No funding since 2016 — the pivot is self-funded.
- **2016:** spun out the non-profit arm — **The Tipping Point in Education** (thetippingpoint.org.gr) — taking the mentoring model free to Greek public schools, esp. remote/island schools. 67k+ students, ~1,000 schools, 3,000+ mentors to date. Yiorgos is co-founder/board/tech partner. This matters: TTP is where the summit's "public-good, nation-building" DNA comes from.
- **~2021 pivot:** education → enterprise Generative AI adoption. Tagline now: *"Adopt Generative AI. Drive team-level adoption. Fuel organizational impact."* Three business lines:
  1. **GenAI Academy** — paid bootcamps/masterclasses/certification for enterprises; "Build AI-First Teams" program (academy.100mentors.com) whose graduates present on the Summit stage. Claims 50+ companies supported, 3,000+ employee graduates.
  2. **Wiserwork** (wiserwork.ai) — the AI meeting agent SaaS. The actual product they ship.
  3. **GenAI Summit** — "the largest GenAI event in SE Europe" (they claim largest in Europe solely dedicated to GenAI). 5th edition: 16 Nov 2026, SNFCC, 5,000+ attendees, 1,200+ companies, 200+ speakers, 4 stages, ~70 sessions. Under the auspices of the **Greek Ministry of Digital Governance**, supported by **EIB, European Parliament & Commission offices in Greece, HDB, HDBI**. Closing fireside with **PM Kyriakos Mitsotakis**.
- **Headcount:** ~17 on the public team page; LinkedIn data says 30–40. HQ Athens; legal entities in UK/US; staff across Greece/Netherlands/US/Cyprus/UK. **Yiorgos relocated to San Francisco in 2026** — they're pushing Wiserwork at US customers while staying "extremely frugal, SE European style" (his words, explicitly framed vs "well-funded US rivals").

### Why an ex-edtech produces a 5,000-person AI summit
Not a side hobby — it's the **go-to-market engine**:
- Academy sells enterprise AI training; the Summit IS the top-of-funnel and the credibility artifact ("the room where the decisions get made").
- The mentoring DNA transfers 1:1: their core competency was always **curating networks of experts and staging structured encounters** (mentor↔student then, speaker↔enterprise buyer now). Same skill, bigger market.
- Self-described as *"trust infrastructure for GenAI adoption across the region"* — a "12-nation corridor that can reach Athens in under two hours." It's positioning Athens (and themselves) as the SE-Europe AI gateway.
- Manifesto language: *"produced from the inside, by people shaping GenAI in practice… It's not a stage. It's a working session."* — this is the judging ethos in one sentence.

## 3. Wiserwork — read the product, read their taste

wiserwork.ai · platform at platform.wiserwork.ai · "powered by 100mentors" · **hackathon partner** (likely credits + mentors on site).

### Thesis (their own words)
*"Meetings are broken. We have thousands of 10x individuals. Still no 10x teams. Your team doesn't lack intelligence anymore. It lacks alignment and direction."* → The product is explicitly **anti-personal-notetaker**: every competitor (Otter/Fireflies/Granola class) optimizes the individual; Wiserwork optimizes the *team's shared output*.

### Architecture of the product (what they chose to build)
| Agent | Job |
|---|---|
| **Curator Agent** | Guided chat → structured agenda in <3 min (before) |
| **Moderator Agent** | Real-time meeting guidance, timekeeping per topic (during) |
| **Project Manager Agent** | Recap, decisions, action items, searchable + time-stamped (after) |
| **AskWiser** | Chat over entire meeting history → answers *or final artifacts* ("draft the follow-up email"). Free while in beta |
| **SkillBase™** | Save how your A-player works (PRD structure, client-recap tone, handoff checklist) → becomes the team's default output template. *"Built on your first principles, so it's hard to argue at the output level"* |
| **MCP server** | Exposes meeting knowledge via Model Context Protocol to **Claude, GPT, Cursor** — "your meetings become context for specs, code and campaigns," permissions enforced at the source |

Surfaces: Google Calendar + Outlook sync; joins Meet/Teams/Zoom with participant consent; webhooks (Business tier). Platform features named like a multi-agent system, not a transcription tool.

### Pricing — the tell
**Per meeting-minute, never per seat. Unlimited users on every plan.**
- Starter €50/org/mo (900 min) · Team €83 (1,800 min) · Business €150 (3,600 min) · Enterprise custom. Overage €0.10/min. Free trial = full Business.
- Site's own calculator frames it against per-seat rivals (12 seats ≈ €216/mo "typical per-seat tool").
- Signal: pricing **is** the thesis — team-level value, adoption friction removed, monetizing work-done not access.

### Security posture (they sell to enterprises)
GDPR, explicit consent flow, "confidentiality by default," "zero data leakage architecture," fully private chats, per-meeting permission control. They think about governance as a feature, not a compliance page.

### Miltiadis Zeibekis — the builder judge
CTO & co-founder since Sep 2014 (~19 yrs experience). Ex-**Atypon** senior software engineer (scholarly-publishing platform, Athens). JavaScript-heavy web apps, decision-making UX, performance. Listed as "Zeimpekis" in the 2017 Envolve filing — same person, transliteration drift. Hires PMs for wiserwork with "builder's mindset, customer-obsessed, data-driven." If he evaluates your project he will mentally trace the pipeline — reliability, latency, and consent/permission handling are where he'd probe.

### Their summit workshop
**"wiserwork | How AI First Teams Run Meetings"** — ticket-holders only, time TBC: *"how intelligent meeting agents capture decisions, coordinate next steps, and turn discussions into actionable deliverables."* It is literally Yiorgos's Panathēnea talk productized. Attend if you have a ticket — it's the taste manual delivered live.

## 5. What the Wiserwork build tells you about their taste

They ship a multi-agent product daily. Judge like builders, not sponsors:

1. **Agents are plural and role-scoped.** Wiserwork isn't one agent — it's Curator + Moderator + PM + AskWiser, each owning a phase. A hackathon entry architected as *specialized agents with clear job boundaries* maps to how they think.
2. **The deliverable is the unit of value.** Not notes, not summaries — *finished artifacts* a team builds on. Demo ending in a polished artifact (spec, PR, brief, reconciled ledger) > demo ending in chat text.
3. **"10x teams not individuals."** Anything multi-user/shared-state/alignment-producing echoes their core bet. Solo-assistant clones = the category they explicitly reject.
4. **MCP-native.** They built an MCP server into their own product and name-check Claude/GPT/Cursor on the homepage. An agent that *consumes or exposes MCP tools* speaks their native tongue — potentially the single highest-signal technical flourish for this org specifically.
5. **Skills/first-principles encoding (SkillBase).** Capturing an expert's workflow and making it the team's default — a project with a "teach once, reuse forever" mechanism flatters this directly.
6. **Consent, permissions, governance as product features.** They sell "permissions enforced at the source," GDPR, consent prompts. A demo that visibly *handles* consent/access (rather than ignoring it) reads as enterprise-mature to them — and matches the summit's "deployment, ROI, governance" theme.
7. **Frugal craft pride.** They compete with "well-funded US rivals" on speed and taste, not budget. Scrappy-but-finished beats grandiose-but-hollow. Cost math shown live lands twice (them + Pastas).

## 6. What impresses this room (synthesis for project choice)

The hackathon's own judging line — **usefulness, craft, aura points** — plus Wiserwork's product religion converges on:

> **An agent (or small agent team) that takes a genuinely tedious, multi-step organizational job and returns a finished, beautiful, team-shareable deliverable — live, end-to-end, with visible tool use and honest boundaries.**

Specific winning shapes for THIS jury:
- **Meeting/coordination-adjacent but not a meeting notetaker** (don't clone their product — flank it): e.g., agent that turns a hackathon team's chaotic Discord/whiteboard session into a decided roadmap + delegated tasks. Uses their mental model without competing.
- **Alignment artifacts**: decision logs, PRDs, RACI, "what did we actually agree" extractors — their AskWiser/SkillBase logic applied to a new surface.
- **MCP-flavored demos**: your agent exposes itself via MCP or orchestrates MCP tools; name-drop that it runs inside Claude/Cursor workflows — literally their feature page.
- **"Teach once" mechanic**: encode an expert workflow → reusable skill/skillfile; aligns with SkillBase AND Oumi's own-the-model thesis (cross-scores with judges.md TIER 1).
- **Quantified loop**: time-saved / error-rate / cost-per-deliverable numbers on the demo (Pastas, Koukoumidis, Kaparounakis all reward this).
- **Consent-gated action**: agent asks before acting on shared data; governance theater that's real, not a slide (Efstathiou's theme, Wiserwork's security page, enterprise sponsors' brief).
- **Aura**: Yiorgos's vocabulary — "beautiful," "execution-ready," "easy to build on top of." End the demo with the artifact, not the transcript.

Kill list for this org specifically: per-seat/individual-productivity framing, "notes" as the output, ignoring who has access to what, slideware, overclaimed autonomy, anything that smells like a thin API wrapper (Zeibekis + Kaparounakis will smell it).
