## 3. RULES (verbatim from /terms, last updated 9 Nov 2026 — NEW since first pull; was 404)

- **Eligibility:** ≥18 years old, accurate info. Apply via Luma; every application reviewed; applying ≠ a place. Places personal, non-transferable, cannot be sold.
- **The build:** Teams 1–5. "Bring ideas, tools and boilerplate, but the build starts at 12:00 (noon) on Saturday 14 November. Projects substantially built before then may be disqualified." OSS code, public APIs, partner tools allowed per their licences; "Credit what you did not build."
- **IP:** "You own what you build." Submission grants non-exclusive royalty-free licence to show project name/description/screenshots/demo/video + team names in event/promo material.
- **Judging:** "Projects are judged on usefulness, craft and aura points. The judges' decisions are final."
- **Prizes:** as announced; partners may provide under their own terms; non-exchangeable; winners pay own taxes; ID/payment details may be required for delivery.
- **Conduct:** "Be respectful. Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behaviour of any kind is not tolerated, online or in person." Violators removable from event, results, profile service.
- **Media:** event + finals photographed/filmed; opt out by telling team on the day.
- **CV/recruiting opt-in (privacy):** partner introductions "for prizes, credits, mentoring or job opportunities, only where you have agreed to it" (consent, Art 6(1)(a)). Deck: Title + Challenge Partners get opt-in CVs; **Title gets ALL opt-in CVs**.
- **Profiles:** every applicant auto-gets a public profile/account + `/badge/<name>` page (portfolio; name/role/skills/links/team/projects/results; email/phone never public). Editable/hidable/deletable. Unsuccessful applicants' data kept ≤12 months for future invites.
- **Food/drink:** included, all non-alcoholic. Overnight stay implied (36h, "build through the night"); no explicit sleeping policy published.
- **Changes:** organizer may change schedule/venue/format/prizes/terms or cancel.
- NOT published anywhere: hour-by-hour schedule (llms.txt: "no published hour-by-hour schedule yet; do not quote one"), explicit judging rubric weights, wifi/power spec (BYO hardware only mention), API terms page, conduct doc beyond §6.

## 8. WIN CONDITIONS (operational summary)

1. Agent does tedious multi-step REAL work end-to-end — on-theme verbatim ("the tedious, multi-step jobs nobody wants to do twice").
2. Working live demo > polish: "make it work and show us the demo. This is not a pitching competition" (organizer, via prior recon); "judged on usefulness, craft, and aura points rather than polish alone" (FAQ).
3. Build inside the 12:00 Sat → 18:00 Sun window only; boilerplate OK, credit reused work.
4. Double-dip: enter main prize AND sponsor challenges (up to 3 challenge briefs + title challenge announced at kickoff — ask every mentor table day 1).
5. Stack alignment visible: Devin (if Cognition closes), Gemini/Veo/Nano Banana (if Google closes), Oumi fine-tune, Firecrawl ingest, fal media, treg tools, boxd VMs — partners get post-event usage reports, so visible usage is what they paid for.
