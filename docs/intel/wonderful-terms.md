# Wonderful Track Terms — verified live 2026-11-14

Source: `hackathon.polymath42.com/terms/wonderful` (HTTP 200, "Last updated October 28, 2026"). The Nov-12 "IP landmine" watch item — resolved. Full text fetched; key clauses below verbatim.

## The IP clause (§4.1) — what it actually says

> "Wonderful shall be and remain the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest, including all intellectual property rights, in and to the Hackathon Output. Participant hereby assigns to Wonderful all right, title and interest worldwide in and to the Hackathon Output … effective upon creation … without further compensation or attribution to Participant."

**"Hackathon Output"** = "everything you build **within the Platform** for the Wonderful Track … including agents, workflows, prompts, configurations and other materials created within the Platform … including any output the Platform generates for you."

**Your carve-outs (same clause):**
- You keep all rights in your own ideas, anything owned before the Hackathon, and **anything you create OUTSIDE the Platform** — but Wonderful gets a license to them solely as incorporated into the Output.
- You "remain free to develop your ideas independently" — provided you don't use/copy the Platform, the Output, or Wonderful's materials.
- You may showcase the Solution in your portfolio and present it at the Hackathon + GenAI Summit.

## Verdict for track selection

- **Build inside the Wonderful platform → Wonderful owns it.** On-platform agents/workflows/prompts/configs are theirs, assigned on creation, worldwide, commercializable.
- **Build outside the platform → it's yours.** The assignment only reaches "within the Platform" output; your own repo, your own stack, your own infra = untouched.
- If the idea has standalone value and you'd build it regardless → **don't give it to the Wonderful track.** Use Open/Content/fal track and keep the repo.
- If you pick Wonderful anyway: keep core logic + eval harness in YOUR repo, use the Platform only for the demo surface — the thinner the on-platform layer, the less you assign.
- Terms can change unilaterally (§10) — re-check `/terms/wonderful` at kickoff Sat before committing.
