# Panathēnea — Deep Dossier (Gen AI Summit Hackathon co-host)

*Compiled Nov 2026 · Sources: panathenea.org/.com, Luma (luma.com/x1wxi5sn, luma.com/rquttffe), Kathimerini/eKathimerini, Dataconomy, The Future Media, Fortune Greece, Startupper/BusinessNews, athenslifetechpark.com, LinkedIn*

---

## 1. What it is

Non-profit "reimagination of the ancient Greek festival" — tech + art + startups, city-wide format (not a conference). Run **entirely by students and recent grads**, all in their 20s, with a heavyweight mentor bench. Self-described pillars: **Connection · Competition · Celebration** — explicitly "competitions with real, valuable prizes, not symbolic ones."

| Edition | Dates | Main venue | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2025** (debut) | 7–9 May 2025 | Athens Conservatoire (Ωδείο Αθηνών) | ~3,100–3,500 attendees, 44 countries, 1,300 founders + 600 investors, 34 side events. Sold out 5 days early. Built in ~4.5 months. |
| **2026** | 27–29 May 2026 | **Zappeion Megaron** + Aigli Zappeiou | 11,500 registrations / ~10k attendees, 60 countries, ~30% international, 3,000 startuppers, 1,500 investors, **90+ side events**, ~440 staff incl. volunteers. |
| **2027** | 26–28 May 2027 | TBA | Pre-reg open |

Format: mornings = keynotes/workshops/startup pitching competition at main venue; afternoons/evenings = 90+ city-wide side events (rooftops, museums, galleries); closes with street party under the Acropolis. Speakers 2026 incl. ElevenLabs, Reflection, Runway co-founders, Deel/Bolt/Airwallex C-levels, Sequoia/Index/Atomico partners, OpenAI EMEA startups lead. 2025 pulled **Melanie Perkins + Cliff Obrecht (Canva)** and Ke$ha.

## 3. Hackathon / competition track record — what they reward

### LifeHack Athens @Panathēnea 2026 — the DIRECT template for this hackathon
36-hour hackathon, **27–28 May 2026, Athens LifeTech Park, Spata — the same venue** as the Gen AI Summit Hackathon. Official Panathēnea side event, co-run with ALTP × Endeavor Greece × Longevity Hacks. 60 participants, 13 teams, 22 mentors, teams of 3–5, solo registrants matched on Day 1, full catering, sponsor-defined tracks, **winners pitched the next day on the Athens Life Sciences Day stage** — the exact same architecture (hackathon at ALTP → finals on a big Monday stage).

Three sponsor tracks, three winners:

| Winner | Track (sponsor) | What they built | Prize |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MetNav** | Metabolic diseases (Pfizer CDI Thessaloniki) | AI tool fusing blood tests + CGM + genomics + microbiome → metabolic-disease risk estimate | €3,000 cash + Microsoft for Startups cross-track Technology Prize |
| **RNAgents** | Drug discovery (ALTP) | **AI agent that generates AND evaluates thousands of candidate siRNAs/ASOs** — literal "agent at work" | 6mo free lab/office at ALTP; first team ever to go hackathon→wet lab |
| **Dyania Challengers** | Clinical AI (Dyania Health, "Docathon vs Synapsis AI") | Model detecting MASH at preclinical stage from unstructured medical data | iPad + 1-month paid internship each |

**Pattern in what won:** all three were *working AI systems with real domain depth* — multi-modal data fusion, agentic generate-and-evaluate loops, clinical NLP on unstructured data. No gimmicks; serious problems, quantified outcomes, a credible continuation path (lab incubation, internships). Microsoft was tech partner (Azure credits up to $150k, A100/H100s, AI Foundry).

### Startup Competition (their flagship "Competition" pillar)
- **2025 winner: AgroU** — agrifood workforce platform (source/hire/pay workers, "fairness, dignity, real impact"). Prize: $325k Microsoft services/credits + €25k cash (Visa Innovation Program) + hoper helicopter ride + marble award carved from **Acropolis-quarry marble** (by tremeEats). Jury: Sofia Oikonomou (Tenity), Ido Jonathan Engel (Microsoft), Simon Schaefer, Nikos Petrakis (Visa GR), Stephanie Hannon, Christian Dorffer, Sakis Tanimanidis.
- **2026 winner: Gig Possible** — gig-work "labor infrastructure" app; "Living CV" (kills static PDF resumes), pay-transparency compliance, kills undeclared work. 53k users + 250 companies in 9 months, zero ad spend. Founders: brothers Lazaros & Giannis Thomas. **"Moved both the crowd and the jury."** Prize: €25k Visa cash + $250k Microsoft credits. Competition drew 100+ entries/20 countries, 6 finalists incl. AlongWave (satellite marine-weather AI) and Circuits Integrated Hellas (satellite-comms semiconductors).

**Competition taste verdict:** both flagship winners are **work/labor-infrastructure plays** — squarely aligned with the "Agents at Work" theme. Criteria pattern: real traction or real working system, social-mission undertone (dignity in work, transparency), stage presence that moves a crowd, and prizes designed to *continue* the project (credits, incubation, internships — they buy a follow-on story, not a photo op).

## 8. Implications for winning (this crew's vote)

1. **Stage-worthy demo is their entire job** — Panathēnea owns the Monday SNFCC finals moment. Give them a live run with visible stakes (real emails sent, real docs filed, graceful error recovery), a clean story arc, and a memorable name/visual identity. They reward "moved the crowd" (Gig Possible won partly on emotion).
2. **Work/labor-infrastructure framing flatters them** — both flagship winners (AgroU, Gig Possible) are "Agents at Work" for human work. Frame the agent as dignifying/augmenting work, not replacing people.
3. **Continuation path > one-off demo** — their prizes are designed as fuel (credits, incubation, internships). Show what the project becomes after Sunday; RNAgents went hackathon→lab.
4. **Sponsor-track fluency** — LifeHack tracks were sponsor-defined with sponsor-specific prizes; expect the same (their sponsors judge their own briefs). Microsoft-for-Startups-style cross-track "technology prize" precedent exists.
5. **Real system depth** — every LifeHack winner fused multiple real data sources/models; surface slop won't survive a Koukoumidis-adjacent jury anyway.
6. **Youth-energy match** — org team is all 20s, Slush-modeled; playful-but-real ("aura" resonates), anti-corporate-slideware.
